# Structure: causality, expectation, and readable movement

## Mainline

The mainline answers “what story is this?” in terms of a protagonist pursuing a present desire through characteristic action under opposition. Background experience should explain the desire and behavior. Supporting threads serve the mainline by complicating, enabling, reframing, or pricing that pursuit; they are not automatically useful because they are interesting alone.

Test every arc and chapter against three variables:

1. desire: what outcome is being pursued now;
2. strategy: what the viewpoint character does about it;
3. pressure: what consequence or opposition changes the available choices.

If none moves, the unit is probably setup without drama, repetition, or a detached subplot.

## Promise -> progress -> payoff

Promises come from tone, genre, viewpoint attention, stated goals, planted objects, rules, relationships, and unresolved disturbances. Progress must be observable and correctly signposted. A payoff fulfills, transforms, or consciously refuses a promise with sufficient causal preparation.

Audit each promise:

- What exact expectation did the text create?
- What evidence tells the reader it is advancing?
- Has the story repeated the same beat instead of changing state?
- Is the planned payoff proportional to waiting and cost?
- Can the reader trace preparation afterward?
- If the promise changes, did the text renegotiate it?

## Nested story threads

Use MICE as a diagnostic lens, not a compulsory formula:

- milieu ends when the character exits or establishes a new relation to the place;
- inquiry ends when the question is answered;
- character ends when identity/dissatisfaction reaches a new equilibrium;
- event ends when disrupted order is restored, replaced, or accepted.

Nest threads deliberately and usually close inner threads before outer ones. A chapter can be treated as a small box inside an arc and volume. Every opened question needs a tracked closure, transformation, or explicit abandonment.

## Causal scene chain

For adjacent units, replace “and then” with “therefore,” “but,” or “meanwhile because.” A strong transition carries at least one causal pressure: consequence, obligation, pursuit, deadline, discovery, resource change, relationship reaction, or plan revision.

Scene/chapter card:

```text
entry state -> immediate goal -> obstacle -> strategy
-> turn -> choice/cost -> exit state -> next pressure
```

Coincidence may start trouble; it should not repeatedly solve it. A reveal must change interpretation or choice, not merely add trivia.

## Pacing

Fast pacing is a high rate of meaningful state change with low confusion, not permanently short sentences, fights, summaries, or skipped consequences. Slow down when the reader must feel a cost, understand a decision, reorient in space, or revise a relationship model. Speed up by removing repeated setup, compressing low-choice travel/training, entering later, exiting after consequence, and combining beats that change the same state.

## Information order

An information jump occurs when each word is recognizable but the combined meaning lacks prerequisites. For every unfamiliar concept:

1. identify the minimum fact needed for the current decision;
2. connect it to a known category, contrast, function, action, or consequence;
3. demonstrate the rule before relying on a complex exception;
4. defer history and taxonomy until they change a choice;
5. verify with a reader who lacks the author's world model.

Do not explain unknown A solely through unknown B and C. A named concept creates an explanation debt; if it never changes plot, image, choice, or payoff, delete or simplify it.

## Try-fail and variation

Early attempts can fail or succeed with a complication; later attempts should demonstrate learning and create earned progress. Vary strategy, cost, knowledge, opponent response, and relationship consequence. Repeating the same failure at a higher numerical scale is not escalation.

## Structure deliverable

Deliver a one-line mainline, promise ledger, nested arc map, causal chapter chain, state-change table, information prerequisites, pacing rationale, and explicit closure conditions for each active thread.
