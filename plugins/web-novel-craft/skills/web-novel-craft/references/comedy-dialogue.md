# 喜剧机制与对白潜台词

## 喜剧 beat

```yaml
comedy_beat:
  setup: facts establishing an expectation
  assumed_pattern: what the reader predicts
  misdirection: optional alternate frame
  punch: shortest pattern break or reveal
  tag: optional incremental beat after the laugh
  callback: prior beat id or null
  character_consequence: changed status, plan, information, or relationship
  voice_scope: narrator or character authorized to make the observation
```

程序：建立可理解的 setup；确认读者能形成单一主预期；用反差、类比或模式打断产生 punch；只在已成立的笑点后加 tag；将 callback 连接到人物记忆或局势；删除作者解释并检查笑点仍能被解码。类比两端必须都真实可理解，笑点必须服务人物、冲突或节奏。舞台现场的 laughs-per-minute 与试演反馈不能机械迁移到小说。

## 潜台词卡

```yaml
dialogue_exchange:
  speaker: char-id
  surface_intent: literal social action
  true_goal: desired outcome
  concealed_fact_or_feeling: ''
  power_before: who can impose cost
  information_advantage: []
  tactic: evade|probe|threaten|appease|perform|misdirect
  physical_behavior: observable contradiction or reinforcement
  listener_inference: what can reasonably be inferred
  state_change: information, power, plan, or relationship
```

程序：先写字面目标，再标真实目标、权力与信息差；让人物通过选择词、回避、物件、动作或话题转换表达；确保听者有足够上下文作合理推断；场景结束必须改变至少一个状态。潜台词不是任意猜心：疲惫、沉默或礼貌都要有文本因果，不能自动解释为愤怒或欺骗。

证据锚点：`nj9MAv9INCY` 00:03:41–01:09:39；`hMMpmlraoDo` 00:00:21–00:10:35。前者主要来自单口喜剧教学，迁移的是机制而非现场指标。
