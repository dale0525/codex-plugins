# Revision: repair the highest-level broken promise first

## Diagnose before editing

Preserve the intended reader contract unless the user approves changing it. Describe the defect with evidence from the text, its reader effect, likely cause, and smallest adequate repair. Separate fact, interpretation, and preference.

Revise in descending leverage:

1. reader contract and premise;
2. mainline, causality, ending, and promise/payoff architecture;
3. character desire, choice, arc, relationship, and POV;
4. chapter/scene state change, information order, pacing, and transitions;
5. paragraph clarity, sentence action, rhythm, diction, and copy errors.

Line editing before structural decisions creates sunk cost and hides the real defect.

## Promise-debt audit

Inventory signals created by tone, genre, attention, goals, questions, objects, rules, and relationships. Mark each open, progressing, paid, transformed, or abandoned. For overdue items, choose:

- add observable progress;
- pay it off proportionally;
- merge it with a stronger promise;
- renegotiate it visibly;
- remove or reduce the setup;
- explicitly abandon it with consequence.

Never rely on the author knowing an answer that the reader cannot see moving.

## Causality and state pass

For each chapter, compare entry and exit state. Mark the choice and cost that caused the change. If two adjacent units connect only with “and then,” add consequence, pursuit, reaction, obligation, or plan revision; otherwise cut, combine, or reorder.

## Information-jump pass

Circle unfamiliar terms and compressed inferences. For each, list prerequisites the intended reader actually has. Repair with a known category, function, contrast, action, consequence, or a brief definition. Avoid explaining unknowns with unknowns. Remove facts that create no decision or payoff.

## Character and POV pass

Check motive before decision, decision before consequence, and consequence before claimed change. Look for passive protagonists, one-note motives, universally competent heroes, frozen supporting characters, arbitrary antagonist behavior, knowledge leaks, unearned viewpoint switches, and voice that depends only on catchphrases.

## Progression pass

Check whether each reward changes option space and each escalation changes more than magnitude. Verify rules, costs, counters, opposition response, meaningful failure, and character interpretation. Consolidate noisy statistics or abilities that never alter decisions.

## Classic-clarity prose pass

Assume the reader cannot see the author's mental model. Revise toward concrete actors and actions:

- remove throat-clearing, apology, self-reference, and repeated summary;
- replace unnecessary nominalizations with an actor plus a strong verb;
- prefer concrete image, action, and consequence over abstract labels;
- delete dead metaphors and institutional boilerplate;
- keep technical terms only when precision or world experience needs them;
- read aloud for syntax, rhythm, accidental repetition, and breath;
- use a cold read or real reader to expose the knowledge curse.

“Show, don't tell” is not absolute. Summary is useful for compression, transition, repeated activity, and low-choice time. Dramatize the moments where perception, action, cost, or relationship must be experienced.

## Regression check

After revision, rerun promise, causality, information, character, progression, and continuity checks. A local improvement fails if it contradicts existing text, steals a later payoff, changes voice, or erases a needed rule.

For genre-specific work, rerun the relevant relationship, clue-fairness, comic/subtext, climax/ending, or historical-evidence check. For AI-generated text, compare original and revision directly, reject unsupported new facts, and judge it by the same character, causality, prose and reader-effect standards as any other draft.

## Revision deliverable

Deliver a prioritized diagnosis, evidence excerpts, intended reader effect, revision plan, before/after samples only where useful, continuity implications, unresolved choices, and acceptance tests. Do not rewrite the whole work unless requested.
